Privacy policy

What this app is. gmail-declutter is a personal tool used by one family. It reads the Gmail inboxes of the family members who have authorized it and produces a daily summary for them.

What it accesses. With each person's permission it reads messages and labels in their Gmail account and may apply or remove labels and archive messages, using the Gmail API scope gmail.modify.

Where the data goes. Nothing leaves the household. Messages are processed on a single home computer and are not stored beyond what is needed to produce the summary. The summary is sent only to the account owners. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access. Any authorized person can remove the app at any time from their Google Account's third-party access settings, which immediately stops all access.

Contact. Questions can be sent to the support email shown on the app's Google consent screen.

Google API Services usage: this app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
